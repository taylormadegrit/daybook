Running Daybook on a Laptop or Clean Windows Install?
Heavy local models can strain laptop CPUs, heat up your machine, and drain battery. Daybook gives you the best of both worlds: use Google Gemini (Free) & Claude for instant cloud responses with zero hardware requirements, or lightweight 1.5B local models for offline privacy.
Recommended for Laptops

✦Google Gemini (Cloud)

Runs in the cloud with zero GPU required. Fast, free, and works out of the box in Chat and in-app web apps without downloading gigabytes of models.

Autonomous AI Coding

✳Claude Code CLI

Anthropic's autonomous coding tool. Daybook installs Node.js LTS and Claude Code CLI in 1 click with zero configuration.

100% Offline & Private

◆Local AI (Ollama)

Runs locally on this PC. For laptops, Daybook selects lightweight 1.5B models (~1 GB) so your laptop stays cool.
